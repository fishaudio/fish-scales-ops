# GEMM performance — sm_90 (NVIDIA H200)

Frozen reference numbers for the block-scaled GEMM paths on sm_90. Protocol,
FLOPs conventions, shape families, M grids and clock policy: [`../README.md`](../README.md).
Tables are generated from `tests/baselines/` jsonl — do not hand-edit rows.

## Provenance and status (2026-10-01 release run; the earlier sessions below are history)

Every table in this file comes from the fish-scales-ops 0.2.0 release perf run
of 2026-10-01 (run `/data/bench-runs/fso_release_perf_20261001/h200/`, report
`REPORT_stage1.md` there), 10:29:03–10:41:57 UTC, on the local eight-H200 box
`39-gcp-h200-2`, GPU 6 (PCI `CB:00.0`, `GPU-f7556d6e-…`, pinned by UUID). The
card ran at its natural clock, with no lock and a 200 ms sampler recording its
clock and power (`../README.md` §5), under the cold-weight protocol of
`../README.md` §1 (copy cap 256, every row at or above 2 × the 60 MB L2). The
environment is the apex-0520 venv: torch 2.13.0+cu130, whose cuBLAS 13.1.1.3
runs the BF16 column, with the sm_90 kernels JIT-compiled in-process by the
NVRTC 13.0.88 that ships in the torch wheel (a probe confirmed that the
process maps it). The build is the fish-scales-ops 0.2.0 release build
(commit `633f5ca`), a clean `ARCH=9.0a` build of the read-only release tree.

**What changed against the 2026-09-29 tables.** The tables these replace came
from the run `/data/bench-runs/h200_unlocked_20260929/` on the same card at its
natural clock, under the `.dev` interpreter (torch 2.11.0+cu130, cuBLAS
13.1.0.3, the sm_90 kernels JIT-compiled by NVRTC 13.2.78) and the round-2
development tree of 2026-09-29, which was squashed into `537efa5` on master.
Of the 412 dense cells (the unpublished `gate` and cubic rows included), 167
read more than 1 % slower in the release run. The release report attributes
every one of them, and none to the code:
- 137 FP8 cells to the NVRTC build. Compiled with NVRTC 13.2 instead, the same
  release kernels return each of them to within 1 % of its 2026-09-29 value
  (median residual 0.0 %).
- 17 cells to the 700 W power cap.
- 2 BF16 cells to the torch and cuBLAS versions: the `wqkv_gated` shape
  (N = 9216, K = 2048) is 2.1 % slower at M = 64 and 4.2 % slower at M = 2048
  under cuBLAS 13.1.1.3.
- 11 cells to noise.

The NVRTC term is a property of the production toolchain. NVRTC 13.0 emits
3.6–14.7 % more instructions per kernel than 13.2 at the same register count,
and on the cells it moved it costs a median 3.1 % at decode (0.9–7.3 %) and
2.0 % at prefill, up to 12.3 % on the N = 2048, K = 4096 projections at
M = 256 (`../README.md` §8).

**The 2026-09-29 tables (history).** They had replaced, on the same day, cold
tables taken under a clock lock (`-lgc 1980`, 1830 MHz sustained). Against
those, the natural clock had moved the dense prefill rows at M ≥ 2048 by +2 to
+9 %, because the boosted card runs into its 700 W limit sooner and is held
lower, and the small-M dense rows by less than ±1 %. The sessions below are the
older history of these tables and of the NVRTC binding; their numbers are
superseded.

The 2026-09-05 sm_120 changes (UE8M0 weight scales by default, K % 128 for
block-FP8, grouped cascade rule v5) do not touch this arch: the sm_90 path
already required only K % 128, its deep_gemm kernels take FP32 scales as
before, and the Python weight quantizer keeps FP32 scales on sm_90.

Cold weights (all tables in this file since 2026-09-29): the captured graph
rotates through R copies of the weight (R × bytes ≥ 2 × the 60 MB L2, up to
256 copies for the smallest projections) and reports replay time / R, so no
row is served from L2; under the earlier single-copy replay the M ≤ 128 rows
had been (10–50 MB of weights fit the L2) and their µs undercut the HBM floor
(`../README.md` §1 and §8). The rotation also amortises the ~2 µs
`cudaGraphLaunch` gap that protocol charged to every cell, so the sub-4 µs
cells read lower than the warm tables did.

Power cap on the dense prefill rows. At its natural clock the card runs every
decode cell at 1980 MHz, but from M = 512 up the dense cells push it into the
700 W limit: the SW power-cap flag appears in 8 of 14 worker windows at
M = 512, 12 of 15 at M = 1024, 14 of 15 at M = 2048 and in every window at
M = 4096 and 8192, where the clock fell to 1440–1620 MHz. Those dense rows are
power-bound numbers rather than clock numbers; this is what the card does in
serving, and it is what the policy publishes. They also depend on the card's
recent power history: the bench times BF16 first and FP8 right after it, the
timed segment lasts only 0.01–0.2 s, and identical repeats of a capped cell
spread widely (Family A `gate_up` BF16 at M = 2048 read anywhere from 277 to
311 µs in the release report's repeats). Do not accept or reject a change on
those rows without a clock sample of both arms, and keep the compile path the
same in both arms: in capped cells a kernel loaded from a warm JIT disk cache
read slower than one compiled in the same process.

dtypes on sm_90: BF16 = unquantized reference (`torch.nn.functional.linear`);
BSFP8 = block-scaled FP8, 1×128 activation / 128×128 weight scales, FP32
K-major scale layout, vendored deep_gemm JIT kernels (dense and grouped).
`µs` is the CUDA-graph replay median (50 replays × 3 repeats); `cos` is cosine
similarity vs the BF16 result on the same inputs; TFLOPS = `2·M·N·K / µs`.
