Frozen reference numbers for the MXFP8 attention kernels on sm_120 (contiguous
prefill, paged decode, paged prefill / extend). Protocol, FLOPs convention and
clock lock: [`../README.md`](../README.md). Tables are generated from
`tests/baselines/` jsonl — do not hand-edit rows. No comparison columns.

## Head configurations

The attention tables use the head geometry of the same three model families
as the GEMM tables (see `../README.md` §3):

| family | H_q | H_kv | D | layers with this attention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Qwen3-4B | 32 | 8 | 128 | all 36 |
| B — Qwen3-30B-A3B | 32 | 4 | 128 | all 48 (heads from public config — verify) |
| C — Qwen3.5-35B-A3B | 16 | 2 | 256 | the 10 full-attention layers only; the 30 Gated DeltaNet layers are not attention GEMM work and are out of scope |
